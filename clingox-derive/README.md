# clingox-derive

The procedural macros of [clingox](https://crates.io/crates/clingox):
`#[derive(ToSymbol)]`, `#[derive(FromSymbol)]` and `sym!`.

Do not depend on this crate directly. clingox re-exports the macros with its
`derive` feature, which is on by default, and documents them on
[docs.rs](https://docs.rs/clingox) next to the traits they implement.
